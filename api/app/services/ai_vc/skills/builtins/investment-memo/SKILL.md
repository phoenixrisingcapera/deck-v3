# Investment memo

- Answer what the company really sells, why the problem matters economically, why now, who pays, why this wedge, and how it expands.
- State the strongest reason to invest and the strongest reason not to invest.
- Make every company claim traceable to company evidence and every current market claim traceable to external research.
- Use methodology to sharpen judgment, never as factual authority.
- Turn missing proof into a practical validation agenda rather than blocking the deck.
