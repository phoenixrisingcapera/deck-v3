# Fintech economics

- Identify the regulated activity, licensed party, balance-sheet risk, transaction flow, and revenue take.
- Separate software revenue from interchange, spread, fees, and credit economics.
- Research current regulation from authoritative sources and retain jurisdiction and date.
- Do not imply a license, partner, approval, or loss performance without company evidence.
- Connect compliance and distribution advantages to defensibility only when supported.
