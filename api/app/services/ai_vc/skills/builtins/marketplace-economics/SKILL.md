# Marketplace economics

- Identify each side, the constrained side, matching frequency, liquidity threshold, and reason to stay on-platform.
- Separate GMV from revenue and revenue from contribution margin.
- Test acquisition loops, geographic density, trust, and disintermediation risk.
- Use verified company evidence for actual liquidity or transactions.
- Frame network effects as hypotheses until the company demonstrates them.
