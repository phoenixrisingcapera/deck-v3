# Investment committee critique

- Record strengths, concerns, missing proof, likely objections, weak claims, and recommended changes.
- Treat even critical-for-fundraising findings as advisory.
- Do not block publication or suppress generation because the company is early.
- Improve the narrative where evidence permits and expose the remaining question internally.
- Never place critique notes, rubrics, or internal warnings in the investor deck.
