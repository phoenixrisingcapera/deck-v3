A structured alternative or companion to `positioning`. Where `positioning` argues differentiation in prose, the Strategy Canvas argues it visually: a value-curve chart over category factors, with the ERIC delta — which factors the team *Eliminates*, *Reduces*, *Raises*, and *Creates* relative to incumbents. Most often used by startups breaking from category conventions; occasionally by emerging managers showing how their fund repositions versus peer funds.
