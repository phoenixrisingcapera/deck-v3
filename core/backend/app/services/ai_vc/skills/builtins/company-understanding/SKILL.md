# Company understanding

- Treat the uploaded deck as evidence, not as a manuscript or slide outline.
- Identify product, buyer, end user, workflow, wedge, business model, proof, metrics, and missing proof.
- Separate current facts, management projections, hypotheses, and editorial instructions.
- Prefer the most specific current source fact; memory is historical context and never overrides it.
- State uncertainty directly. Do not repair gaps with plausible startup language.
