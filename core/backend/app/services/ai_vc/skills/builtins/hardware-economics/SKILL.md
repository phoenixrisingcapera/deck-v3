# Hardware economics

- Separate prototype performance from manufacturing and commercial readiness.
- Examine bill of materials, gross margin, yield, certification, lead times, installation, maintenance, and working capital.
- Make milestone and capital requirements explicit.
- Treat supplier, certification, and production claims as company facts only when sourced.
- Show how software, data, service, or distribution can improve otherwise constrained economics.
