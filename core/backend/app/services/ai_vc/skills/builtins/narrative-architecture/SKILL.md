# Narrative architecture

- Ignore source slide order and source slide count.
- Define the investor beliefs that must be established, then assign one clear job to each slide.
- Lead with the strongest credible thesis; sequence problem economics, wedge, proof, market, model, expansion, and financing logic as the company requires.
- Omit unsupported standard sections rather than manufacturing content.
- Choose a comprehensive but disciplined length and explain why every slide exists.
