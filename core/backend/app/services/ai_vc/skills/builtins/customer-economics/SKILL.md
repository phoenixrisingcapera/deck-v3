# Customer economics

- Name the buyer, user, budget owner, and economic unit separately.
- Trace the current workflow and the cost of delay, fragmentation, labor, leakage, or risk.
- Use company evidence for achieved outcomes and external evidence only for category benchmarks.
- Distinguish willingness-to-pay hypotheses from observed pricing or contracts.
- Select the few economics that materially change investor belief; do not fill a slide with generic benefits.
