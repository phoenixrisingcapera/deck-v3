# Why now

- Require a dated external source for every current market or regulatory assertion.
- Connect the shift to adoption, economics, distribution, or product feasibility.
- Reject timeless slogans such as digitization is accelerating unless supported and decision-relevant.
- Distinguish a genuine inflection from a general trend.
- Omit why-now claims when credible current evidence is unavailable.
