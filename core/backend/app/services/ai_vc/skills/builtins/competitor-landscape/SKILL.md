# Competitor landscape

- Compare the job owned, buyer, workflow boundary, data position, distribution, and switching cost.
- Include manual work and bundled incumbent modules as substitutes.
- Explain where each alternative stops and why the proposed wedge can enter.
- Do not claim superiority without company evidence; frame unsupported advantages as strategic hypotheses.
- Prefer a strategic landscape or workflow boundary over a decorative logo grid.
