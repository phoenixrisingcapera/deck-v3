# SaaS economics

- Separate recurring software revenue from services, implementation, and pass-through spend.
- Examine retention, expansion, gross margin, CAC payback, sales cycle, onboarding, and support intensity.
- Treat category benchmarks as external comparisons, not company performance.
- Explain why the initial workflow creates a durable system of record, engagement, or intelligence position.
- If retention or willingness-to-pay proof is missing, make it an internal objection and a next milestone.
