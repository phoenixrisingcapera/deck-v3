Shared archetype. Distinct from `problem` (which is about pain) and `market-size` (which is about scale) — `why-now` is about timing: the inflection that makes this the right window. In a startup deck it rides between problem and solution; in a VC deck it rides between fund-thesis and the credibility slides.
