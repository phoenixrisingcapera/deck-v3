## What this slide is

Positions against the obvious alternatives without disparaging them — they are good at a different job.

## Why it's here

Pre-empts the first question any buyer asks. The answer has to be structural, not a feature comparison.

## What to surface

- Incumbents are built for institutional diligence at volume: screen a thousand, shortlist fifty
- This job is a personal conversation or a team meeting
- One organization framed for one person's actual motivation
- The leap requires knowing the funder; the tools don't

## Visual hierarchy suggestion

Two-column contrast — 'built for' vs 'this job'. Avoid a checkbox matrix; that invites feature argument and loses the structural point.
