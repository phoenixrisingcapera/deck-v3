## What this slide is

The historical reason to agonize over backend choice was migration cost. Coding agents remove it.

## Why it's here

The second hinge. This is what earns permission to start before deciding, and it is the least obvious argument in the deck.

## What to surface

- Old world: pick wrong, pay for years
- Now: agents write the SQL and transforms, round-trip, aggregation layer to custom app and back
- A quarter of integration work becomes an afternoon of supervised agent work
- Maturing into scheduled or event-driven data fluidity — no migration event
- Therefore: deciding later, with usage data, is strictly better than deciding now
