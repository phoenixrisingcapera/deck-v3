## What this slide is

The inventory of existing assets that make a 2–3 month timeline credible rather than optimistic.

## Why it's here

- Augment It — microservices already wired to SearXNG, Exa AI, Firecrawl, SerpAPI
- SurrealDB multi-modal architecture, already in production use
- A vetted shortlist of more powerful augmenters (Crawlbase and peers) — lossless.group/toolkit/tag/Data-Augmenters
- Domain starter from the Reach University work
- Self-host stack already standing; n8n adds integrations and agent-driven data flows
- Reuse across ai-labs and content-farm; Svelte throughout, Tauri for native desktop and mobile
- Closing line: assembly and refit against a domain we already know — not invention
