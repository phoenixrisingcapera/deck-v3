## Why it's here

Every deck earns its ask. This one is deliberately small at the front — the only thing requiring a decision today is starting Track A, which costs nothing.

## What to surface

- Timeline: 2–3 months of dedicated work
- $5,000 at inception
- $5,000 on completion
- Token, agent-harness, and SaaS expenses passed through from The Lossless Group to Palmer AI

- Defer the subscription question until there is usage data

The "start now" band on top, weighted largest — it is the only ask that needs an answer in the room. Three equal terms cards beneath (timeline, inception, completion), then three quieter cards — pass-through expenses, maintenance, and the deferred subscription question. The money should read as matter-of-fact, not as the headline.
