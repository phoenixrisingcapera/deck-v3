## What this slide is

The custom application is not a proposal to start building. Inventory of what exists in Augment It today.

## Why it's here

Collapses the perceived risk and timeline of 'custom app'. Also the credibility beat — this is a system, not a slideware promise.

## What to surface

- Enrichment passes with triage and accept-back review
- Canonical entity layer: organizations, people, affiliation edges
- Organization workbench — work an org's links, streams, corpus, people in place
- Corpus curator — build a strategy/thesis corpus, live multi-operator sync
- Pluggable web research across multiple providers
- Already holds a substantial set of education funders from the reach-edu work
- Missing: impact framing, funder-matching, audience-targeted output. Not the engine.

## Visual hierarchy suggestion

A capability inventory — dense but scannable. The education-funders line gets its own emphasis; it is the strongest single fact on the slide. End with the honest 'what's missing' line.
