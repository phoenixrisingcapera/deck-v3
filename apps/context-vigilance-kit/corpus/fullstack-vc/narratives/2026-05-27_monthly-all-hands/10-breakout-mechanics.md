## What this slide is

The mechanics of the breakout. Five questions per track, one shared one-pager artifact. Same shape across all three tracks.

## Why it's here

Right before sending people into breakouts, the audience needs to know what they're being asked to do.

## What to surface

- Five questions, numbered, ordered by the cognitive arc (real → ideal → evidence → personal gap → commitment)
- The one-pager artifact template — four fields a group fills in the last 90 seconds
- 12-minute time budget

## Visual hierarchy suggestion

Two columns: left = questions (numbered Q1-Q5), right = artifact template (a sketched one-pager). Q5 visually accented since it's the load-bearing closer. Could underline the "willing AND able" phrase to telegraph the cohort split that's coming.
